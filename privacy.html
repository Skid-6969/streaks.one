PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how streaks.one ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and software. By using streaks.one, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

  • Discord account data — if you join our Discord server, we may see your username, ID, and messages you send there.
  • License data — if you purchase access, we store the license key and basic order details needed to validate your access.
  • Local data — the software may store settings and usage data locally on your device.

We do not collect passwords, payment card numbers, or other sensitive financial information directly; payments are handled by our payment provider.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve streaks.one
  • Validate licenses and grant access to the software
  • Provide support through our Discord server
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized use

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with service providers necessary to operate streaks.one (such as payment processing and license validation), or when required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed to provide the service, validate licenses, and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time via our Discord server.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children

streaks.one is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, reach out to us in our Discord server.
